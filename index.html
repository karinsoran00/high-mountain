About us

High Mountain – Built on Trust, Driven by Excellence

Established in 2012, High Mountain is a diversified company operating across local and international markets, with a strong foundation in the petroleum industry, international trade, and commercial operations.

Over the years, High Mountain has built a reputation for reliability, professionalism, and a commitment to excellence. Our operations encompass a growing network of petroleum stations, alongside international trading activities specializing in the import and export of petroleum products, oil-related goods, and other commercial commodities.

Through our extensive business activities, we connect local and international markets, facilitating cross-border trade, developing strategic partnerships, and ensuring the efficient movement of goods across global supply chains.

Driven by a forward-thinking approach, we continuously seek opportunities to expand our market presence, strengthen international relationships, and diversify our business portfolio while maintaining high standards of quality, integrity, and operational efficiency.

At High Mountain, we believe that sustainable growth is built on trust, strong partnerships, and a dedication to delivering value. With more than a decade of experience, we remain committed to expanding our reach, supporting economic development, and establishing ourselves as a trusted partner in both domestic and international markets.

High Mountain — Connecting Markets. Building Trust. Delivering Excellence.
